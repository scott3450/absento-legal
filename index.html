Questions that come up

Why don't I mark the classes I attend?

Attending is the default. Absento counts every scheduled class as attended unless you mark it skipped, so on days you miss nothing you don't need to open the app.

A class was cancelled, or there is a holiday.

Open the course and mark that session Cancelled. For a holiday, add it in Settings ▸ Holidays. Cancelled sessions and holidays drop out of the count, and a percentage rule is recalculated from the sessions that are left.

I had a medical report for several days.

Use “+” ▸ Mark days… on the Courses screen, choose the date range and Excused. Whether an excused absence counts as a skip is set per course, in the course form.

Some rows were missing after importing a screenshot.

Text is read on your iPhone, and a blurred or cropped row can be missed; Absento tells you how many rows it couldn't read. Add the missing times from the course form, or press and hold on the Timetable to draw a class in.

What is Absento Pro?

A one-time purchase. It unlocks ten more themes, each with its own app icon, the Joker icon, and the This Week widget. Everything else stays free: tracking, reminders, importing, the other widgets and Siri. There is no subscription.

I bought Absento Pro but it shows as locked.

Open Settings ▸ Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. This also brings Pro back after you reinstall Absento or move to a new iPhone.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Absento Pro is refunded, a locked theme falls back to Cobalt; your courses are not affected.

The widget says “No courses yet.”

The widget shows a summary that Absento writes each time something changes. Open Absento once and the widget follows.

Can I use it in Turkish?

Yes. Absento follows your iPhone language; English and Turkish are included.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Absento keeps track of the classes you miss and counts how many more you can skip. Everything it needs to do that stays on your iPhone.

What Absento stores

  • Your courses: name, code, attendance rule, weekly times and rooms.
  • The sessions you mark: skipped, excused, cancelled.
  • Your semester dates and holidays.
  • Your settings: theme, appearance, app icon and notification choices.
  • Whether you own Absento Pro, as a single yes or no.

Where it is stored

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. A short summary (your courses, the skips left and this week's classes) is also written to a shared app group container so the widgets can draw it. That container is on your iPhone too, and only Absento and its widgets can read it. Like any app's data, it is included in your iPhone backup if you back up your device.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing. Absento has no servers, no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no networking code, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Calendar

Absento asks for calendar access only when you choose Import from Calendar. It reads your events once, on your iPhone, to suggest courses from events that repeat every week. It writes nothing to your calendar and keeps only the courses you choose to import.

Photos and files

When you import a timetable from a screenshot or photo, you pick the images with the system photo picker; Absento sees only the images you pick. Their text is recognised on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework, and the images are not saved. Calendar (.ics) and timetable PDF files you choose are read the same way, on your iPhone.

Purchases

Absento Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Absento never sees your payment details, name or email address. To know whether Pro is unlocked, Absento asks the App Store on your iPhone for your purchase, verifies it there, and keeps only the yes or no. The purchase record itself is kept by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications and Siri

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled and delivered by your iPhone; no notification server is involved. Siri and Shortcuts actions run inside Absento on your iPhone; Apple's own privacy policy covers how Siri handles your voice.

Deleting your data

Delete a single course from its screen. Deleting Absento removes everything it stored, including the widget summary.

Children

Absento collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
